Beetle logo, Fredoka, and other ways to show status

Interface study for proposal-cozy-06 (see aesthetics.md). Two variants were compared; Minimal was picked, Enamel is kept for comparison. Same screen as state S01 in concept/v3_layout.html (#15 waits). The scene is a flat placeholder.

Changed from the earlier cozy studiesBeetle on the green plate as the logo. Fredoka for the logo, panel title and notification; Figtree stays for the interface. Pills, tabs, badges and buttons have 4–6 px corners instead of full rounds.
StatusEnamel: stamped plant tags on every row, and a water gauge that drains while an agent waits. Minimal: a growth track of five pips, seed to bloom. More options compared at the end.
KeptLayout and sizes, terminal, bookmarks, the hotbar on keys 1–6 and 0, colours.

The mark

The agent beetle, cut down: shell, head, four legs, one leaf. The seam is a lens, one line that splits and joins again (branch and merge). On the plate it is one colour plus the seam; at night the seam glows amber from inside.

EnamelMinimalStatus options

Enamel

Rows stay enamel plates. Status is a stamped plant tag with a hole, like the tags stuck in real pots. The waiting row trades its tag for a water gauge that drains, so you can see how long it has been thirsty without reading the timer.

Minimal

No plates. Each row has the plant in a circle, a word and a growth track: five small squares for seed, sprout, leaf, bud and bloom. Filled squares show how far the agent has come; the current one blinks while it waits. The hotbar shows a shape marker per slot, so status reads without colour.

Other ways to show status

Six ways, each shown for growing, waiting, error, review and merged, in day and night. They combine: one carries the meaning, a second adds shape for colour-blind readers.
