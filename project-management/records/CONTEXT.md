# Records

What we decided and how the work went. Records are history: add new files, don't rewrite old ones. If a decision changes, write a new record that points to the old one.

## What goes here

- Decisions. The format will come from the decision-recording skill; until then, a short dated file with what was decided, why, and what else we considered.
- Meeting and standup notes: what we discussed, what we agreed, who does what.
- Work log: what happened on a day, if it is worth remembering for the final presentation.
- Retrospectives: one per week or milestone. What went well, what didn't, what we change.
- Scope changes: when and why the plan changed. Also update `scope.md`.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD_<short-name>.md`, for example `2026-10-05_project-setup.md`. Subfolders per type only once there are enough files to need them.
