- Moved the branch graph from an overlay over the scene into a **Branches** tab of the side panel, with a status dot on each branch label.

- Took screenshots of every state and both styles for a presentation: `concept/v3_layout-slides/`.

## Proposed, not decided

- Windows sit in fixed slots and are sized by buttons only (minimise, maximise, focus this, close). No dragging.
- The scene always fills the page; the camera frames the pots into the free area with a view offset.
- Maximise applies to all visible windows; entering focus mode closes the side panel, leaving it restores the panel.
- Every terminal is an agent. A shell tab in an agent's worktree can come later.
- Side panel: Agents, Issues, Branches, one fixed width of about 400 px.
- Issue list is the MVP entry; 3D seed packets come later. Priority flag dropped.
- In the prototype: clicking a pot replaces the visible terminal, shift-click or "Open beside" adds a second one. Not yet in the layout page.
